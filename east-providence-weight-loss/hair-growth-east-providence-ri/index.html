    How Hair Growth Treatment Works in East Providence, RI

    Hair Loss Treatment for Men in East Providence

    Most male hair loss is androgenetic alopecia. DHT, made from testosterone, gradually miniaturizes follicles in genetically sensitive men, starting at the temples or crown. The two main medicines are finasteride, which cuts DHT, and minoxidil, which lengthens the growth phase. Combined use tends to work better than one alone.

    Hair Loss Treatment for Women

    In women, thinning usually shows up as a wider part or less volume on top, while the front hairline stays put. Pattern loss is the usual reason, though low ferritin, thyroid trouble and postpartum shedding can mimic it. Minoxidil is the main FDA-approved option for women. Off-label oral options, such as spironolactone, are added when appropriate.

    Finasteride + Minoxidil in East Providence

    Finasteride is an FDA-approved 1 mg daily tablet for male pattern hair loss, and it lowers scalp and blood DHT by roughly two-thirds. Minoxidil, FDA-approved as a topical, keeps follicles in the growth phase longer so strands come in thicker. The pairing is the standard. A compounded combination spray is another route, though it is not FDA-approved.

    Dutasteride + Minoxidil

    Dutasteride blocks both forms of the enzyme that makes DHT, so it lowers DHT by more than 90 percent in the blood. It is FDA-approved for an enlarged prostate and used off-label for hair loss, most often in men who did not get enough from finasteride after a year. It lingers. Because it stays in the body for months, providers screen more carefully. Minoxidil rounds it out.

    Minoxidil + GHK-Cu in East Providence

    Some people want a topical-only plan with no DHT blocker. In this blend, minoxidil drives regrowth and GHK-Cu, a copper peptide, has early research suggesting it may help the scalp environment. It is compounded. It is not FDA-approved. Women and finasteride-averse men use it most.

    How a Provider Chooses Between Treatments

    Your provider weighs sex, age, pattern, speed of loss and medical history. Expect questions about family plans, libido concerns, mood history and pills versus topicals. Men with typical pattern loss often start on finasteride and minoxidil. Women usually start with minoxidil.

    What to Expect Over Time in East Providence

    Hair grows about half an inch a month. Visible change takes three to six months, and full results take about a year, so patience is part of the plan. Early shedding on minoxidil is common and usually temporary. Stopping treatment lets the loss resume.
